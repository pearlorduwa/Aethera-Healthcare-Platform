Menstrual Health

What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle prepares the body for pregnancy. Most cycles last between 21–35 days.

Common Symptoms

  • Cramps
  • Bloating
  • Headaches
  • Mood changes
  • Fatigue

When to See a Doctor

  • Heavy bleeding
  • Severe pain
  • Periods lasting longer than 7 days
  • Missed periods without pregnancy

Women's Health Conditions

PCOS

A hormonal condition that may affect periods, ovulation and fertility.

Endometriosis

A condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside it.

Fibroids

Non-cancerous growths that develop in or around the uterus.

Myth vs Fact

❌ Myth

You shouldn't exercise during your period.

✔ Fact

Light exercise may help reduce cramps and improve mood.

❌ Myth

Only adults can have PCOS.

✔ Fact

PCOS can begin during the teenage years.

Frequently Asked Questions
